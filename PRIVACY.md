# Privacy Policy

Cue Duel does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app only uses the network for online games that you host or join yourself. The moves of the game are passed on by a small relay server on paperstream.dev, without being stored. The list of open games is also kept there, with the game name and a lock flag, and removed when the game ends.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Settings, statistics and replays are saved only on your device and are deleted
  when you uninstall the app.
- The app uses two permissions: internet access for online games and vibration for haptic feedback.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/LucasTHCRR/CueDuel/issues
