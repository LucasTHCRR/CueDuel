# Privacy Policy

Cue Duel does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app only uses the network for online games that you host or join yourself. It talks directly to the other phone. If that is not possible, the moves of the game are passed on by a small relay server on paperstream.dev, without being stored. The list of open games is also kept there, with the game name, a lock flag and the host address, and removed when the game ends. Your address is shown only to you.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Settings, statistics and replays are saved only on your device and are deleted
  when you uninstall the app.
- The only permission the app uses is vibration, for haptic feedback.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/LucasTHCRR/CueDuel/issues
